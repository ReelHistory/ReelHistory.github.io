About this edition

Reel History is available to read free online as a series of scans of the book’s original pages.

The original ebook included brief audio excerpts from Juma’s Archive in each chapter. Pages 6, 106, and 260 featured film excerpts of the Aboriginal Music Society performing live in the early 1970s.
